Kshitish Chandra Hati is a PhD student and Senior Research Fellow at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Pune, supported by a CSIR fellowship. He joined the laboratory in January 2021, following the completion of his BS-MS degree from IISER Kolkata in July 2020.

His research interests lie at the interface of organic synthesis and bioconjugation chemistry, with a primary focus on developing covalent protein bioconjugation strategies. His work involves the design and synthesis of novel chemical tools and methodologies for the selective modification of proteins, with an emphasis on expanding the scope and efficiency of covalent bioconjugation approaches.

Beyond the laboratory, he enjoys watching and following sports and has a keen appreciation for food and exploring different culinary experiences.
