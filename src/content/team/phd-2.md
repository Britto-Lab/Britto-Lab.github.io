Ashwini Khyade is a Ph.D. student and Senior Research Fellow at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Pune, supported by the INSPIRE Fellowship. She holds an M.Sc. in Organic Chemistry from Punyashlok Ahilyadevi Holkar Solapur University, Solapur. Her research lies at the interface of organic chemistry, bioconjugation chemistry, and nanomedicine, with a primary focus on developing protein- and lipid-based nanomedicines for cancer therapy.

Her work involves the design and synthesis of molecular probes and prodrugs, development of bioconjugates, purification and characterization of biomolecular systems, and their biological evaluation through in vitro and in vivo studies. By integrating chemical synthesis with nanomedicine and biological evaluation, her research aims to develop innovative and targeted therapeutic strategies for cancer treatment.

Beyond the laboratory, she enjoys trekking, travelling, creative arts, and writing poetry.

**Research Interests:** Protein-based nanomedicines • Lipid-based nanomedicines • Cancer therapeutics • Targeted drug delivery • Bioconjugation • Prodrug and probe design
