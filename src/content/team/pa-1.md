Harsha Babaso Kamble is a Project Assistant at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Pune, working in the area of synthetic organic chemistry. She obtained her Bachelor's degree in Chemistry from Shivaji University, Kolhapur, followed by a Master's degree from Savitribai Phule Pune University, Pune.

At IISER Pune, her research focuses on organic synthesis and multi-step organic synthesis, with an emphasis on reaction optimization, purification, and characterization of organic molecules. Her work involves the synthesis of functional organic intermediates and molecular building blocks relevant to biomedical applications.

Beyond research, she enjoys reading books and exploring nature, which provide her with opportunities to learn, reflect, and appreciate the world beyond the laboratory.
