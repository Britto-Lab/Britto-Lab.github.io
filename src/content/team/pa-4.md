She completed her BS-MS degree with Chemistry major in 2026 from IISER Bhopal. Her research interests lie at the interface of synthetic organic chemistry and chemical biology, particularly in bioconjugation, molecular probes, and targeted drug delivery. Her previous research experience includes the synthesis of tetrazine-functionalized probes for bioorthogonal protein labelling and photochemical applications, as well as heterocyclic synthesis and reaction optimization. She enjoys exploring how chemistry can be used to develop innovative molecular tools for biological and biomedical applications and is always curious to learn new techniques and approaches.

Beyond the laboratory, Vaishnavi enjoys reading, writing poetry, watching movies, and travelling. She finds joy in discovering new places, connecting with people, and embracing experiences that help her see the world a little differently.
