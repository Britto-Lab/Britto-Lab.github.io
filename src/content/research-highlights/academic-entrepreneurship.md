The mission of universities and academic institutes has changed in the 21st century. Top universities around the world have created an ecosystem (tech transfer offices, incubation centers, etc.) to convert basic discoveries into societal impact and economic value. In this regard, my group is developing next-generation antibody-drug conjugates for effective treatment of cancer. To my knowledge, most Indian biotech/pharma companies are working on developing biosimilars (copycat products developed after patent expiry), and none of them are working on developing novel ADCs from scratch. We are first Biotech Company from India to work on this niche area.

2. **2025** - Secured seed funding of Rs 5 Lakhs from IISER-Pune AIC SEED
3. **2025** – Participated in Boots Camp along with my PhD Student
4. **2024** - Biotechnology Ignition Grant (BIG, DeepTech Startup Grant) – Qualified for Final Round
